<p class="eyebrow">SILICON DEBUGGING / TEAM PROJECT</p>

DeepDebug uses language models to help engineers investigate failures during silicon validation. I built it with **Vivek Chokkalingam** during our AMD internship.

## The engineering problem

When a system under test fails, the hard part is knowing what to check next. DeepDebug analyses the system state and suggests where to look.

## My role and the public result

I was a co-developer and presenter on the two-person project. DeepDebug received **Best Overall Project at AMD's Intern Innovation Showcase**, and we later presented the work to Dr. Lisa Su.

This overview covers the publicly announced purpose, collaboration, and recognition. The implementation is not an open-source project, and no internal architecture, datasets, or performance figures are published here.

## Why it belongs in my portfolio

The project connects two interests that run through my work: understanding hardware behavior and building tools that make difficult systems easier to investigate. It also represents collaborative engineering and communicating a technical idea to an engineering audience.
